About Me

Who I am and what I do

My name is Kripal Prajapati and I live in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. I work as a Data Analyst, and I am also learning and building in Data Science and Machine Learning. I got interested in data because I like finding out what is really going on behind the numbers, and that curiosity is what keeps me practicing every day.

I started with Python and Excel, then moved on to SQL and Power BI. After that I started working on statistics and machine learning. Along the way I have put more than 38 projects on my GitHub. Some of them are small practice programs and some are full dashboards and analysis projects, like customer churn, customer behaviour and student performance.

Most of my time goes into cleaning messy data, exploring it in Jupyter Notebook, and showing the results in a simple dashboard that anyone can understand. I also train basic ML models, such as the Titanic survival prediction, to learn how prediction works step by step.

I am looking for a role where I can work with real data, learn from a good team, and keep improving my skills. If you have a project or an opening, please contact me below.
